Our Story

We are a home for good food, great coffee and meaningful connections. Kalahari Café was created to bring people together with hospitality rooted in community.

Locally Sourced

We use carefully sourced ingredients to support our farmers and preserve the authentic flavour of the region.

Community First

Each visit is a taste of the landscape, with dishes and coffee crafted to reflect the natural beauty and warmth found across the Kalahari.
